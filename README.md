## Aim

To write, execute, and evaluate Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques (Straightforward Prompts, Tabular Format Prompting, Missing Word Prompting and Preceding Question Prompting) for an AI-powered chatbot that helps customers with product troubleshooting, order tracking and general inquiries in a conversational, user-friendly tone.

## Scenario

An e-commerce company, **ShopEasy**, wants a chatbot that can:

- Resolve product troubleshooting issues
- Track orders and explain delivery status
- Answer general questions (returns, warranty, payments)
- Keep a friendly, polite and conversational tone

---

## Prompt Types Explained

| Prompt Type | What It Means | When It Helps | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Straightforward Prompt** | A direct, clear instruction with no extra framing | Quick FAQs, short steps | "List three steps to fix a phone that will not charge." |
| **Tabular Format Prompt** | Asks the model to organize the answer in rows and columns | Comparisons and multi-attribute info | "Compare Standard and Express delivery in a table by time, cost and tracking." |
| **Preceding Question Prompt** | A guiding "why/what" question comes first, so the model reasons about the cause before answering | Empathetic, explanatory answers | "Why do couriers delay parcels? Reassure the customer and suggest next steps." |
| **Missing Word Prompt** | A template with blanks the model must fill in | Fixed policies, standard messages | "Returns are accepted within ____ days of delivery." |

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

**Prompt 3.1**
```
Why might a warranty claim be rejected? Then explain our 1-year manufacturing warranty and how to submit a replacement request, in a friendly tone.
```
**Generated Output:**
Claims are usually rejected when the damage comes from drops, liquid exposure or repairs by unauthorized shops, since the warranty only covers manufacturing defects. If your product stopped working on its own within the first year, go to My Orders > Request Warranty, upload your invoice and a photo of the issue, and our team will get back to you within 24 hours!

**Prompt 3.2**
```
What are common reasons a courier delays a parcel? Reassure the customer and explain what to do if it is more than 2 days late.
```
**Generated Output:**
Delays often happen because of bad weather, heavy festive-season volume or rerouting at a regional hub, so please don't worry, your order is still on its way. If it is more than 2 days past the expected date, reply with your Order ID and we will open a priority trace with the courier or arrange a replacement.

### 4. Missing Word Prompting

**Prompt 4.1**
```
Complete the policy statement: "Customers can return or exchange an item within ____ days of delivery if it is unused and in its ____ packaging."
```
**Generated Output:**
Customers can return or exchange an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging.

**Prompt 4.2**
```
Fill in the blanks: "I understand how frustrating ____ can be. I have escalated your ticket (#____) to our senior team, who will contact you via ____ within 4 business hours."
```
**Generated Output:**
I understand how frustrating a delayed refund can be. I have escalated your ticket (#SE-7342) to our senior team, who will contact you via email within 4 business hours.

**Evaluation summary**

- **Claude** follows format limits (sentence counts, exact table columns, filled blanks) closely and keeps a warm, natural tone.
- **ChatGPT** is equally accurate and friendly, and gives slightly more complete answers, but often adds extra text beyond what was requested.
- **Gemini** is thorough and well organized, but tends to add extra content or formatting that was not asked for, which lowers format adherence.
- For a customer support chatbot, all three are suitable, and the choice depends on how strictly the replies must follow a fixed format.

### Observations

- **Straightforward prompts** give short, fast answers, ideal for frequent FAQs, but have little depth.
- **Tabular prompts** are the easiest to scan, especially on mobile chat screens.
- **Preceding question prompts** give the most empathetic and reasoned replies because the model explains the cause first.
- **Missing word prompts** give tight control over fixed policies and templates, but the answers are short and depend on the blanks given.
- Policy values such as return days, ticket numbers and prices are example values. A real chatbot must get them from the company's own data.

---

## Conclusion

Prompt structure strongly affects the clarity, tone and reliability of a chatbot's answers. Straightforward prompts suit quick FAQs, tabular prompts suit comparisons and diagnostic guides, preceding question prompts suit empathetic and explanatory replies, and missing word prompts suit standard policies and escalation messages. Combining the four techniques lets a customer support chatbot adapt its response format to each customer's need.

## Result

Thus, the customer support chatbot prompts using Straightforward, Tabular Format, Preceding Question and Missing Word prompting techniques were designed, executed and evaluated successfully with the generated report.
